Search that speaks Bulgarian

Search every meeting by word and jump to the exact moment it was said. It understands word endings: “среща” also finds “срещата” and “срещи”.

Your meetings stay on your computer. The only thing that leaves it is the audio you send to the transcription provider you pick.

How it works 00:02

Three steps, one meeting.

  1. 01 · REC

    Record

    Hit record in Meeting Helper. OBS captures the call with your mic on its own track.

  2. 02 · TEXT

    Transcribe

    Send the audio to Soniox or ElevenLabs. Get a transcript with speakers, then tidy it up.

  3. 03 · FIND

    Search

    Type a word, find every meeting where it came up, and jump straight to that moment.

Before you start 00:03

What you need.

  • Windows, macOS or Linuxx64 and ARM64 builds for each.
